Hi, I’m Alex — an applied mathematician, machine learning scientist, and educator.

I hold a Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics and Statistics from the Colorado School of Mines. My work sits at the intersection of mathematics, statistics, machine learning, and scientific computing, with experience spanning academic research, data science, and scientific consulting.

I am particularly interested in mathematical modeling, statistical learning, inverse problems, optimization, and scientific machine learning. I am open to full-time research, data science, machine learning, and quantitative roles, as well as teaching and selected scientific collaborations.

Research & Scientific Work

My doctoral research focused on computational methods for large-scale problems in physics, including optimal transport, optimal control, inverse problems, generative modeling, and scientific computing. I have also worked with hyperspectral remote-sensing data for geologic inference and currently consult with Denver Life Sciences on statistical modeling of physiological data related to tissue hydration.

Data Science & Machine Learning

Before and alongside my scientific work, I spent several years as a senior data scientist and machine learning researcher at On The Barrelhead, NerdWallet, and Launch Potato. My work included statistical modeling, experimentation, recommendation and ranking systems, optimization, calibration, and machine learning research.

I am most interested in data science and ML roles where mathematical and statistical reasoning are central to the work, particularly problems involving modeling, inference, optimization, experimentation, or scientific data.

Teaching

I also enjoy teaching mathematics, statistics, and data science and am interested in adjunct and other teaching opportunities. My teaching interests include calculus, linear algebra, differential equations, probability and statistics, numerical analysis, optimization, mathematical modeling, and data science.
